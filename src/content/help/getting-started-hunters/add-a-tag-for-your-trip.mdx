Tags are how the state tracks what you can legally take. Most hunts need at least one. Add yours to the wallet so your guide can pull it onto the report — and so you can find it again next season.

## Add a tag

1. Tap **Wallet** in the bottom nav.
2. Tap **Add new card**.
3. Pick **Tag** as the type.
4. Pick the species (the dropdown lists species your state allows).
5. Pick the zone or unit if your state uses them.
6. Type the tag number from your physical tag.
7. Tap **Add wallet item**.

## Linking the tag to a trip

If you added the tag before your guide put you on a trip, link it from the trip page:

1. Open the trip from your dashboard.
2. The **Action needed** card at the top lists the credentials your guide needs (your hunting license, a tag, and so on).
3. On the tag row, pick your tag from the **Use existing from your wallet** dropdown.
4. Tap **Use this**.

The card flips to **Trip credentials** with a **Change** button on each row in case you need to swap.

## After you punch it

When you fill the tag on a hunt, open the tag in your wallet and tap **Mark as tagged out**. The tag stays on file for the report but stops appearing in active dropdowns for new trips. To bring it back into rotation, tap **Mark as active**.
