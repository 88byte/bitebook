Tags are how the state tracks what you can legally take. Most hunts need at least one. Add yours to the wallet so your guide can pull it onto the report — and so you can find it again next season.

## Add a tag

1. Tap **Wallet** in the bottom nav.
2. Tap **Add to wallet**.
3. Pick **Tag** as the type.
4. Pick the species (deer, elk, bear, turkey — the dropdown lists what your state allows).
5. Pick the zone or unit. If your state doesn’t use zones, leave blank.
6. Type the tag number from your physical tag.
7. Tap **Save**.

## Linking the tag to a trip

If you added the tag before your guide added you to the trip, link it now:

1. Tap the trip from your dashboard.
2. In the **Your tags** section, tap **Link tag**.
3. Pick the tag from your wallet.

Your guide can now pull this tag onto the harvest report when they file it.

## After you punch it

When you fill the tag on a hunt, tap the tag in your wallet, then tap **Mark used**. We’ll keep it on file for the report but stop showing it in the dropdowns for new trips.
