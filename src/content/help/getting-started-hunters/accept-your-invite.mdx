Your guide added you to a trip in Bite Book. You got an email or a link. Here’s what to do with it.

## If your guide emailed you

1. Open the email from your guide. It came from `support@lastbite.pro` with your guide’s name in the subject line.
2. Tap the **Accept invite** button in the email.
3. If you don’t already have a Bite Book account, you’ll be asked to set a password. Pick one and tap **Create account**.
4. Confirm your email when the verification message arrives. Your account is now linked to the trip.

If you already have a Bite Book account from a previous guide, just sign in. We add you to the new trip automatically.

## If your guide sent you a share link

1. Tap the link your guide sent (text, group chat, anywhere).
2. The page asks for your email. Type it in.
3. If it’s a new email, set a password. If you already have an account, sign in.
4. You land on the trip detail page.

## What to do next

Once you’re in:

- **Add your hunting license** to your wallet. Your guide needs it to file the state report.
- **Add any tags** the guide asked for, with species and zone.
- **Sign any waivers** the guide attached to the trip. You can do this from your phone.

That’s it. On trip day, your guide handles the paperwork. You hunt.
