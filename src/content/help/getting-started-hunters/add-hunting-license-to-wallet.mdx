Your guide files the state harvest report after every trip. To do that, the report needs your hunting license number on it. Adding it to your wallet once means every guide you ever hunt with already has it on the report.

## Add it

1. Tap **Wallet** in the bottom nav.
2. Tap **Add to wallet**.
3. Pick **Hunting license** as the type.
4. Type your license number.
5. Pick the state that issued it.
6. Set the expiration date.
7. Tap **Save**.

That’s the full list. We don’t ask for anything else — your guide only needs the number, the state, and the expiration to fill in the report.

## Optional: snap a photo

If you want to be able to show your license to a warden from the app, tap **Add photo** on the wallet card. Snap the front of your physical license. The photo is private — only you and the guides you’re actively on a trip with can see it.

## Renewing later

When you renew your license:

1. Tap your license card in the wallet.
2. Tap **Add a renewal**.
3. Type the new number and expiration. The old one moves to **Archived**.

We’ll remind you 60, 30, and 7 days before it expires.
