Your guide files the state harvest report after every trip. To do that, the report needs your hunting license number on it. Adding it to your wallet once means every guide you ever hunt with already has it on the report.

## Add it

1. Tap **Wallet** in the bottom nav.
2. Tap **Add new card**.
3. Pick **License** as the type.
4. Type your license number.
5. Pick the state that issued it.
6. Set the expiration date.
7. Tap **Add wallet item**.

That’s the full list. We don’t ask for anything else — your guide only needs the number, the state, and the expiration to fill in the report.

## Optional: snap a photo

If you want to be able to show your license to a warden from the app, add a photo on the wallet form. Snap the front of your physical license. The photo lives on the wallet card.

## When the license expires or renews

There’s no “renew” button in Bite Book. When you get a new license, archive the old one and add a new wallet item:

1. Open your old license from the wallet.
2. Tap **Archive** to move it out of active dropdowns.
3. Go back to **Wallet** and tap **Add new card** to enter the new license details.
