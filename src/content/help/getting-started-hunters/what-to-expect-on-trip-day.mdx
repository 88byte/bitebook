You don’t need Bite Book in your pocket all day. Your guide handles the paperwork. Here’s what shows up in your account before, during, and after the trip.

## Before you leave

Open Bite Book and check the trip detail page. If anything needs your attention, you’ll see an **Action needed** card at the top of the trip with rows for each missing item. Common rows:

- A required hunting license — pick yours from your wallet via **Use this**, or tap **Add new** to create the wallet item.
- A required tag — same flow, picked or added from the wallet.
- A waiver to sign — tap the document to open and sign it.

When everything’s linked, the card switches to **Trip credentials** with a **Change** button on each row in case you need to swap.

## During the hunt

Bite Book runs in the background. You don’t need to log anything. If you harvest something, your guide marks it on their phone in the hunt log.

You will not see harvests appear on your trip page in real time. Bite Book shows your harvest entry only after your guide generates the hunt report at the end of the trip.

## After the hunt

Two things might happen:

- **Your guide asks you to leave a review.** Stars + a few words. Reviews show up on your guide’s reviews page (averaged across their hunters). Takes 30 seconds.
- **You may be asked to sign a waiver** if your guide attached one and you haven’t signed yet. Tap the document on the trip page, sign with your finger, save.

Hunters do not sign the harvest log itself — that’s your guide’s job. Your only signing is on waivers your guide explicitly attached.

That’s the whole trip from your side. You hunt, your guide files the report, and Bite Book keeps a record on your account.
