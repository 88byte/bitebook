You don’t need Bite Book in your pocket all day. Your guide handles the paperwork. But it helps to know what’s happening behind the scenes so nothing surprises you.

## Before you leave for the hunt

Open Bite Book once and check three things:

- Your hunting license is in your wallet and not expired.
- Your tags for this trip are in your wallet and linked.
- Any waivers your guide attached are signed. You’ll see a yellow chip on the trip if anything’s unsigned.

If something’s missing, the trip detail page tells you what. Fix it before you head out.

## During the hunt

Bite Book runs in the background. You don’t need to log anything — your guide does it. If you do harvest something, your guide marks it on their phone, picks your name, picks the species, and that’s it.

If you want a personal record, the trip page on your phone shows everything your guide logs in real time.

## After the hunt

Two things might happen:

1. **Your guide asks you to e-sign the trip log.** A notification pops up. Tap it, scroll to the signature box, sign with your finger, and tap **Done**. The signed PDF goes back to your guide.
2. **Your guide asks you to leave a review.** This is private — it’s for the guide’s own records. Five stars, a few words. Takes 30 seconds.

That’s the whole trip. You hunt, the guide files the report, and Bite Book keeps a record of every trip you’ve ever taken in case you need to show one to a warden, an insurer, or your future self.
