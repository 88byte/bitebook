You can attach a photo to any wallet item. It’s never required, but it’s nice to have when a warden physically checks your documents and you want to back up that you have the original on file.

## Add a photo to a new item

The photo field doesn’t unlock until the wallet item is saved. Order matters:

1. Tap **Wallet**, then **Add new card**.
2. Fill in the type, identifier, dates, and any other fields.
3. Tap **Add wallet item**. The page bounces to the saved item.
4. Scroll to the **Photo** section and tap **Add photo**. (Until you save, the section reads **Save first, then add a photo** with the button disabled.)
5. Pick from your camera or photo library. Bite Book scales the image down to a max 2000px on the long edge and saves it.

## Add a photo to an existing item

1. Open the wallet item.
2. Find the **Photo** section (it’s at the top for stamps and permits, at the bottom for everything else).
3. Tap **Add photo**.

After upload, the section shows the preview with two text actions: **Replace** and **Remove**. Tap Replace to swap in a new image, Remove to delete the photo (the wallet item itself stays).

## Accepted formats

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, and HEIF. iPhone photos work as-is.

## Where the photo lives

The photo attaches to the wallet item only. It doesn’t automatically appear on a state harvest log PDF or in the warden share view — those use the data you typed in (license number, dates), not the photo. The photo is for your own records and for showing a warden visually that the document exists.

## Stamps and permits get a callout

If you’re adding a stamp or a permit, the Photo section moves to the top of the form with this note:

> Wardens often check the physical document. A photo on file means you can prove it’s in your possession even if you forgot the original.

That’s a hint, not a requirement. Skip it if you don’t want to.
