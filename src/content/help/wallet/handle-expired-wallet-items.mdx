Your wallet shows two side-by-side sections per type: **Active** and **Expired**. Items don’t disappear when their date passes — they just bucket over to Expired. Here’s what that means and what to do.

## What triggers Expired

A wallet item is Expired when its **Valid to** date is in the past. That’s the only trigger — there’s no manual flag to set.

The check runs every time you load the wallet, so the moment your license’s valid-to date rolls past today, it shifts from Active to Expired.

A tag that you’ve already marked tagged out shows as **Done** instead of Expired, even if the date has passed too. Tagged out wins over expired.

## What Expired blocks

An expired wallet item won’t show up in dropdowns when you’re linking a credential to a trip. It’s still on file — your record stays — but it’s out of rotation.

For hunters: an expired license or tag won’t appear in the **Action needed** picker on a trip, so your guide won’t be able to pull it onto a state report.

## There’s no "renew" button

Bite Book doesn’t have a renew action. When your license, tag, permit, or stamp is reissued, you do two things:

1. Open the expired item from your wallet. Tap **Archive** at the bottom of the form to move it from Expired into Archived. The record stays for your audit trail.
2. Go back to **Wallet** and tap **Add new card** to enter the fresh document with the new number and dates.

You can also leave the expired item in the Expired bucket and just add a new active one — both records stay on file. Archiving is for when you want a cleaner deck.

## We don’t send expiration reminders

Bite Book doesn’t email or notify you when a license is about to expire. Check your wallet at the start of each season.

## Deleting

If you want to remove an expired item entirely, you have to **Archive** it first, then open the archived item and tap **Delete**. Delete is permanent.
