Your wallet holds every credential you might need to show in the field or have pulled into a state report. Bite Book has eight item types — pick the one that matches what you’re holding.

## The types

- **License** — your hunting or fishing license
- **Tag** — a species tag (deer, elk, bear, turkey, etc.)
- **Permit** — a state or federal permit (zone, harvest permit, draw permit)
- **Stamp** — a state or federal stamp (duck stamp, upland)
- **Harvest report card** — a state-issued report card
- **Guide license** *(guides only)*
- **Insurance** *(guides only)*
- **Business credential** *(guides only)* — packing course, swift water, first aid, anything else

Hunter accounts only see the first five. Guide accounts see all eight, since guides also hunt.

## Add an item

1. Tap **Wallet** in the sidebar (guide) or bottom nav (hunter).
2. Tap **Add new card**. (You can also tap an empty type tab and use the same button there.)
3. Pick the type from the dropdown at the top.
4. Fill in the fields. The form changes per type — only the fields that matter for what you’re adding will appear.
5. Tap **Add wallet item** at the top of the form (or scroll to the bottom and tap the same button there).

Common fields across types:

- **Identifier** — the number on your card (license number, tag number, permit number, and so on). The placeholder shows an example format.
- **State** — pick from the US states dropdown when applicable.
- **Valid from / Valid to** — the date range printed on your physical document.
- **Notes** — anything you want to remember.

## Tag-specific fields

Tags have a few extras:

- **Species** — free text with a dropdown of common species filtered by hunting vs fishing.
- **Zone or unit** — free text. Leave blank if your state doesn’t use zones.
- **Tag designation** — General, Limited Quota, Premium, PLO, Apprentice, etc.
- **Weapon / method** — rifle, archery, muzzleloader, and so on.
- **Sex restriction** — Male, Female, or Either.
- **Multi-use** checkbox — leave unchecked for a single-use tag (auto-marks tagged out on harvest). Check it for a multi-animal tag like turkey, pig, or predator.

## Stamp and permit specifics

- **Stamps** show a "Signed across the face" checkbox — required for federal duck stamps.
- **Permits** add a Landowner / property field for PLO and similar permits.

## Harvest report cards

Pick the state, the species, and the season. The form also asks for a **Report due by** date — a small reminder that, for example, "CA HRC is due Jan 31 the year after the season."

## Insurance and business credentials (guides only)

- **Insurance** captures the insurer, policy number, per-occurrence and aggregate limits, named insured, and additional insured.
- **Business credential** captures the course or cert name, level, issuing org, and instructor ID.

## Saving

Tap **Add wallet item**. The page bounces you back to your wallet list with the new item in the **Active** section. If anything’s wrong, open it to edit — fields auto-save.
