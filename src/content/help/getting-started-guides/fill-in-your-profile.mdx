Your profile feeds your state’s trip log and the warden share page. Every blank field shows up on the report as a blank field.

Tap **Settings** in the sidebar to edit any of these.

## Identity (required)

- **First and last name.** Your real name. State forms want it spelled the same way it is on your license.
- **Phone.** Optional but recommended — wardens occasionally call.

## Address (required for most state forms)

- **Street, city, state, ZIP.** This is your residential address, not where you guide. Most state harvest reports require it. If you don’t fill it in, every trip log you generate has a blank where the state expects an address.

## Outfitter details (optional)

- **Business name.** Shows on your hunter invite emails and on the trip log if you’re working as a registered outfit. Leave blank if you guide under your own name.
- **State you operate in.** The state you guide in. Used to pre-fill state on new trips and to pick a default state log template at onboarding.

## Account

- **Email.** This is the email you sign in with and where Bite Book sends notifications. Tap **Change email** to update it. We send a confirmation link to the new address — until you tap that link, your old email is still active.
- **Password.** Reset it on the sign-in screen if you forget it.

Tap **Save** at the bottom of any section to commit your changes.
