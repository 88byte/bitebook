Bite Book takes about five minutes to set up. You sign up, run through a short onboarding wizard, and you’re ready to log your first trip.

## 1. Sign up

Go to bitebook.lastbite.pro and tap **Sign up**. Enter your email and pick a password. We send you a verification email — tap the link in it to confirm. Your trial starts the moment you confirm.

You get 7 days free, no card required. After that it’s $9 a month or $90 a year. Cancel anytime in Settings.

## 2. Run the onboarding wizard

After you sign in for the first time, you land on a 5-step wizard. The fields here are the ones your state’s trip log needs — Bite Book can’t pre-fill what’s missing, so it’s worth getting right up front.

The five steps are:

1. **Business basics** — first and last name, business name (optional), the state you operate in.
2. **Guide license** — license number, state, and expiration. Skippable if you want to add it later.
3. **State log doc** — pick a Bite Book template for your state, upload your own log doc, or skip and decide later.
4. **Save signature** — draw your default signature with your finger or trackpad. Required.
5. **Done.**

Tap **Save and continue** at the bottom of each step.

## 3. You’re in

You land on the dashboard. From here you can add a trip, invite a hunter, or upload a doc. Most guides start by adding a trip — it’s the fastest way to see what Bite Book can do for you.

If you closed the wizard early or want to come back to it, your profile fields are also editable in **Settings**.
