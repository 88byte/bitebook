Bite Book takes about five minutes to set up. You sign up, run through a short onboarding wizard, and you’re ready to log your first trip.

## 1. Sign up

Go to bitebook.lastbite.pro and tap **Sign up**. Enter your email and pick a password. We send you a verification email — tap the link in it to confirm. Your trial starts the moment you confirm.

You get 7 days free, no card required. After that it’s $20 a month or $200 a year. Cancel anytime in Settings.

## 2. Run the onboarding wizard

After you sign in for the first time, you land on a 3-step wizard. Don’t skip it — these are the fields your state’s trip log needs, and we won’t be able to fill them in for you later if they’re blank.

The wizard asks for:

- Your first and last name
- The state you guide in
- Your business name (optional — leave blank if you don’t have one yet)
- Your guide license number, state, and expiration

Tap **Save & continue** at the bottom of each step.

## 3. You’re in

You land on the dashboard. From here you can add a trip, invite a hunter, or upload a doc. Most guides start by adding a trip — it’s the fastest way to see what Bite Book can do for you.

If you closed the wizard early or want to come back to it, your profile fields are also editable in **Settings**.
