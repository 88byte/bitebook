Your wallet is where Bite Book keeps every credential you might need to show — guide license, insurance, business permit, anything else. We pull from it when we fill out your state’s trip log, and you can pull it up in seconds when a warden walks up.

If you ran the onboarding wizard, your guide license is already in your wallet. If you skipped that step, here’s how to add it.

## Add the license

1. Tap **Wallet** in the sidebar.
2. Tap **Add new card**.
3. Pick **Guide license** as the type.
4. Fill in the number, the state that issued it, and the expiration date.
5. Snap or upload a photo of the physical card if you have one. Optional — the photo shows up on the wallet card and on the warden share page.
6. Tap **Add wallet item**.

## When the license expires or renews

There’s no “renew” button in Bite Book. When your license is reissued, you create a new wallet item and archive the old one:

1. Open your old license from the wallet.
2. Tap **Archive**. The old record stays on file but stops appearing in active dropdowns.
3. Go back to **Wallet** and tap **Add new card** to enter the new license details.

If you want to delete an old license entirely, archive it first, then open the archived item and tap **Delete**.
