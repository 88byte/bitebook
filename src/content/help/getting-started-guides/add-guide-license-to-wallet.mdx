Your wallet is where Bite Book keeps every credential you might need to show — guide license, insurance, business permit, anything else. We pull from it when we fill out your state’s trip log, and you can pull it up in seconds when a warden walks up.

If you ran the onboarding wizard, your guide license is already in your wallet. If you skipped that step, here’s how to add it.

## Add the license

1. Tap **Wallet** in the sidebar.
2. Tap **Add to wallet**.
3. Pick **Guide license** as the type.
4. Fill in the number, the state that issued it, and the expiration date.
5. Snap or upload a photo of the physical card if you have one. This is optional, but it’s what shows up on the warden share page when you swipe to share.
6. Tap **Save**.

## Renewing it later

Guide licenses don’t get edited when they renew. They get **replaced**. When your renewal arrives:

1. Tap the existing license in your wallet.
2. Tap **Add a renewal** at the bottom.
3. Fill in the new number and expiration. The old one moves to **Archived** so you keep the audit trail.

Bite Book reminds you about renewals 60, 30, and 7 days before the expiration date.
