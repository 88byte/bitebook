The fastest way to learn Bite Book is to add a real trip. End to end takes about five minutes.

## 1. Add the trip

From your dashboard, tap **Add trip**. Fill in:

- **Trip name.** What you want to remember it as. “Fall black bear — Johnson party.”
- **Dates.** Start and end. If it’s a single day, set both to the same day.
- **Location.** State is required (it picks the right harvest log template). City, zone, and county are optional but a few states need county on the report.
- **Activity and species.** Pick from the dropdown. Add more than one if it’s a mixed-bag trip.

Tap **Save**. Your trip lands on the dashboard under **Upcoming**.

## 2. Invite your hunters

Tap the trip you just made. In the **Hunters** section, tap **Invite**. You have two paths:

- **Email invite.** Type one or more emails. Bite Book sends a branded email with a link that signs them in to their own account.
- **Share link.** Copy a single link you can paste into a text or group chat. Anyone with the link can join the trip.

Hunters who already have a Bite Book account get added straight to the trip. New hunters get prompted to set a password and confirm their email.

## 3. Run the trip

On trip day, you don’t do anything different in the app. Bite Book is in the background.

After the trip, fill in the hunt log:

1. From the trip detail, tap **Hunt logs**.
2. Each hunter on the trip already has their own row. Expand a hunter to add their species rows (species, quantity harvested, quantity released, optional notes).

## 4. Generate and sign the state log

When the trip is over:

1. Open the trip and tap **Wrap up trip**, then confirm with **Finish trip**.
2. Open **Hunt logs** again if you’re not already inside. Scroll to **Generate filled PDFs**.
3. Pick the state log template you’ve mapped (mappings are set up per doc at `/app/docs/[id]/mapping`). If you haven’t mapped a template yet, that step has to happen first.
4. Tap **Generate Filled Out Log**. Bite Book builds the PDF using the field mappings you defined.
5. In the **Logs** list below, find the new row and tap **Sign**. Add your signature with your finger or trackpad.

The signed PDF stays on the trip so you can email it, hand it to a warden, or pull it up next year if you ever need to.
