Every new guide account starts with 7 days free. No card required up front. Here’s how it actually works.

## Sign up

When you sign up at bitebook.lastbite.pro and confirm your email, your trial begins. We don’t ask for a card during signup — Stripe is set to "if required," which means you only see a payment form if and when you decide to add one.

## During the 7 days

You have full access to everything in Bite Book — trips, hunters, hunt logs, the wallet, the warden share, all of it. The Settings → Billing tab shows a copper **Trial** pill and a line that reads "Trial ends [date]."

## What happens at the end

There are two paths:

- **You added a payment method during the trial.** When the trial ends, Stripe charges your plan rate ($9 a month or $90 a year — whichever you picked) and your subscription is fully active. No interruption.
- **You did not add a payment method.** Stripe pauses the subscription. Your account becomes read-only — you can still sign in, view your trips and history, and read help articles, but you can’t create new trips, generate logs, or sign anything.

To unpause, go to **Settings → Billing** and add a card. Your subscription resumes immediately.

## Canceling during the trial

You can cancel at any point. From **Settings → Billing**, find the **Cancel subscription** link.

A confirmation modal explains: your subscription stays active until the end of your trial, then your account becomes read-only. You can reactivate anytime before that date with one tap. After that date, you’d have to start a new subscription to keep using Bite Book.

If you cancel during the trial, you’re not charged.

## Switching plans during the trial

You can flip between Monthly ($9) and Annual ($90) at any time during the trial. The new plan takes effect at the end of the trial — no proration is charged because the trial isn’t a paid period yet. See [How to switch between monthly and annual billing](/app/support/switch-monthly-annual).
