Bite Book has one plan with two billing options: $9 a month or $90 a year. You can switch in either direction at any time from your Settings.

## Open the plan switcher

1. Tap **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Open the **Billing** tab.
3. Find the **PLAN** section.

You’ll see two chips:

- **Monthly · $9 / mo**
- **Annual · $90 / yr** with **Save 17%** in copper

The chip for your current plan has a **Current** pill on it.

## Switch

1. Tap the OTHER chip — the one that isn’t marked Current.
2. Bite Book shows a "Calculating proration…" spinner while it asks Stripe what the change costs.
3. A confirmation panel appears with the headline **Switch to Monthly · $9 / mo** or **Switch to Annual · $90 / yr** and a body explaining the math.

The body line depends on where you are in your billing cycle:

- If switching costs money today: "You'll be charged **$X** today. Next renewal: [date]."
- If you have unused time on the old plan: "You'll receive **$X** credit applied to future invoices. Next renewal: [date]."
- If the math nets to zero (common during the trial): "No charge today. The new plan starts immediately and renews on your next cycle. Next renewal: [date]."

4. Tap **Confirm switch** to commit, or **Cancel** to back out.

## When you can’t switch

The plan chips are hidden on a couple of states:

- If you’ve canceled and your subscription is set to end at period close, the section reads "Reactivate first" — you have to reactivate before you can switch plans.
- If you don’t have an active subscription at all, the chips don’t render.

## What changes

Stripe handles the proration the moment you confirm. The new plan starts immediately. Your renewal date in the Billing tab updates to reflect the new cycle.
