Generating a filled state harvest log takes three things: a log doc you’ve mapped, a hunt log you’ve filled in, and the **Generate Filled Out Log** button. Here’s the full first-time path.

## Step 1 — Pick or upload a state log doc

You can do this during onboarding or anytime later.

**During onboarding (step 3 of the wizard):**

1. The step is titled **State harvest log**.
2. The dropdown lists Bite Book templates for your state.
3. Either pick a template (it becomes your default) or pick **I'll upload my own (skip for now)**.
4. Tap **Save default** or **Skip for now**.

**Later from the docs library:**

1. Tap **Documents** in the sidebar, then the **Bite Book templates** tab to browse what’s on offer.
2. To upload your own, switch back to **My docs** and tap **Upload doc**.
3. On the upload form pick **Harvest log** as the kind, set the state if it’s state-specific, attach the PDF, and tap **Save doc**.

If your state doesn’t have a Bite Book template yet, see [What to do if Bite Book doesn't have your state's form yet](/app/support/state-form-not-yet-supported).

## Step 2 — Map the doc’s fields

Every log doc has to have its fields mapped before it can be generated. Bite Book templates come pre-mapped. If you uploaded your own, you map it yourself.

1. From the docs library, open your log doc.
2. Find the **Field mapping** section. The button reads **Set up mapping** if it’s never been mapped, or **Edit mapping** if it’s in progress.
3. Tap it. You land on the mapping wizard.
4. Tap **Start AI mapping**. AI reads the PDF and pre-fills mapping suggestions. Takes about 10–20 seconds.
5. Review each field. The AI is suggesting — you’re approving. Edit anything that’s wrong.
6. When you’re happy, scroll to the bottom and tap **Mapping Complete**.

You can re-run AI suggestions any time with the **Re-run AI** button if you want fresh picks.

## Step 3 — Fill the hunt log on the trip

1. From the trip detail, tap **Hunt logs** (forest-green button in the action row).
2. The page is the **Hunt report** for the trip. Set the **Hunt date** and **Trip purposes** (Hunting, Big game, Fishing, Fly fishing, Other).
3. Each hunter on the trip has their own accordion row. Expand a hunter to add species rows (species, qty harvested, qty released, optional notes).
4. The whole page auto-saves on blur — no save button.

## Step 4 — Generate the filled PDF

1. Below the hunter rows, find the **Generate filled PDFs** section.
2. **Log title** input — what the generated PDF will be named (you can rename later).
3. **Log template** dropdown — pick the mapped log doc to fill. Bite Book templates and your own uploads both show here. Items matching the trip’s state float to the top.
4. Tap **Generate Filled Out Log**.

The new PDF appears in the **Logs** list below with these per-row actions: **Open**, **Download**, **Sign**, **Re-generate**, **Delete**. The PDF is unsigned at this point — see [How to e-sign the harvest log](/app/support/e-sign-the-harvest-log).
