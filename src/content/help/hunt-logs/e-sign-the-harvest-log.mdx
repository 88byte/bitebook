Once you’ve generated a filled harvest log PDF, the next tap is **Sign**. Here’s what happens and what to expect.

## Hunters do not sign harvest logs

Only the **guide** signs the harvest log. There’s no flow for sending the harvest log to your hunters for their signatures.

Hunters can sign **waivers** that you attach to a trip — that’s a different document type, with its own flow on the hunter’s phone. The harvest log is yours.

## Save your default signature first

You drew this during onboarding step 4. If you skipped it or want to change it, go to **Settings** and update your default signature.

The default signature pre-fills the signing canvas the next time you sign a log. You can keep it or tap **Clear** to draw fresh for a particular document.

## Sign a generated log

1. On the trip’s **Hunt logs** page, scroll to the **Logs** list.
2. Find the row for the PDF you want to sign.
3. Tap the **Sign** icon-tile (with a pen-line icon).
4. The **Sign and finalize** modal opens.
5. The canvas pre-fills with your default signature if you have one. Tap **Clear** to draw fresh, or accept it as-is.
6. Tap **Save signature**.

## What signing actually does

Bite Book paints your signature onto the bottom-right of the last page of the PDF and stamps any "Date Signed" fields with today’s date. The signed PDF saves alongside the unsigned copy — both stay available.

After signing, the Logs row shows:

- A green **Signed [date]** pill
- The Open and Download buttons now point to the signed PDF
- The Sign tile flips to **Re-sign**

## Re-signing

If you re-generate the PDF after editing harvest entries, the signed copy is overwritten by the new unsigned copy. You sign it again with **Re-sign**. The modal title flips to **Re-sign report**.
