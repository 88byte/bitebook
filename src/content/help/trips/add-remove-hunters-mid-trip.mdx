Plans change. Someone drops out the day before, a buddy joins on day two, the original group of three turns into four. You can edit a trip’s hunter list at any point — even after the trip has started.

## Add a hunter

1. Tap the trip.
2. In the **Hunters** section, tap **Invite**.
3. Pick how to invite them:
   - **By email** — Bite Book sends a branded link. Best for one or two hunters whose addresses you have.
   - **Share link** — copy a single link to paste into a text or group chat. Best for bigger groups. (See: [Email invite vs share link](/app/support/email-invite-vs-share-link).)
4. Save.

A hunter who already has a Bite Book account gets added to the trip immediately and sees it on their dashboard. A new hunter has to confirm their email and set a password before they’re fully on the trip — the trip page shows them as **Pending** until they do.

## Remove a hunter

1. Tap the trip.
2. In the **Hunters** section, tap the hunter’s row.
3. Tap **Remove from trip**.
4. Confirm.

Removing a hunter from a trip:

- Drops them from the harvest report (you’ll be prompted to remove their harvests too if any were logged).
- Leaves their wallet untouched. Their license, tags, and signed waivers stay in their account.
- Doesn’t remove them from your hunter network. They’re still available to invite to future trips. To take them off your network entirely, see [How to remove a hunter from your network](/app/support/remove-hunter-from-network).

## A hunter who never accepted

A hunter who was invited but never confirmed their account shows as **Pending** in the hunters list. To clean them up, tap their row and pick **Cancel invite**. The invite link stops working immediately.
