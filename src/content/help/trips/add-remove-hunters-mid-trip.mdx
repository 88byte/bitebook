Plans change. Someone drops out the day before, a buddy joins on day two, the original group of three turns into four. You can edit a trip’s hunter list at any point — even after the trip has started.

## Add a hunter who’s already in your network

1. Open the trip.
2. In the **Hunters** section, tap **Manage hunters**.
3. Check the box on each hunter you want on this trip. Uncheck to remove. Changes save as you toggle.

## Add a hunter who’s NOT in your network yet

You invite new hunters from the **Hunters** page (`/app/hunters`) in the sidebar — that’s the account-level invite surface, separate from the trip detail.

1. Open **Hunters** in the sidebar.
2. Use the invite form. The form has two tabs: **Send email** sends a one-shot email invite to a single address. **Get link** generates a multi-use share link you can post anywhere.
3. Once a hunter accepts, they appear in your network and become checkable on any trip via **Manage hunters**.

## Add hunters direct to a trip via a share link

The trip detail page also has a **Trip share link** panel. Tap **Get trip share link**, copy the URL, share it with the group. Anyone who opens it lands directly on the trip after signing in. The link expires on the trip’s start date.

## Remove a hunter from a trip

1. Open the trip.
2. Tap **Manage hunters**.
3. Uncheck the hunter. The change saves immediately.

Unchecking a hunter on a trip leaves their wallet, account, and history untouched — it only severs the trip-side link. To remove them from your network entirely, see [How to remove a hunter from your network](/app/support/remove-hunter-from-network).

## Cancel a pending invite

Pending invites live on **Hunters** (`/app/hunters`), not on the trip detail. Find the row and tap **Cancel invite**. The invite link stops working immediately.
