Wrapping up a trip is what triggers the state harvest report. It also moves the trip out of your **Active** list so your dashboard stays focused on what’s next.

## When to wrap up

After the last day in the field. You can wrap a trip up the same evening or wait a few days while you confirm details with your hunters — Bite Book doesn’t time you out.

## Steps

1. Tap the trip.
2. Make sure every harvest is logged. Each entry needs a hunter, a species, and a method. Date and location notes are nice but not required.
3. Tap **Wrap up trip** at the top.
4. Bite Book asks you to confirm: this changes the trip status from **Active** to **Completed** and unlocks the state report.
5. Tap **Confirm**. The trip is now in **Completed**.

## File the state report

Right after wrap-up, you’ll see a green tile: **Generate state log**. Tap it.

1. Bite Book picks the right form for your state and pre-fills every field it can — your name, address, license number, the trip dates, the hunters and their licenses, the harvests, the methods.
2. Review the filled-in form. Anything in copper is from your wallet or your profile and can be re-pulled. Anything in black is something you typed.
3. Fix anything wrong. Tap **Save**.
4. Tap **Generate PDF**. Bite Book builds the signed PDF and stores it on the trip.
5. E-sign at the bottom. If your state requires the hunter’s signature too, tap **Send to hunters for signature** — they get a notification and can sign from their phone.

The signed PDF lives on the trip detail page forever. Email it to the state, hand it to a warden, or pull it up next year if you ever need it.

## After wrap-up

A wrapped-up trip is mostly read-only. You can still edit the harvests if you spot a mistake, but adding new hunters or changing the dates is locked. If you need to make a substantial change, tap **Reopen trip** at the top of the trip page — it moves back to **Active** until you wrap it up again.
