Wrapping up a trip moves it from In progress to Done. After that, you can still pull up the hunt log, generate a filled-out PDF, and sign it.

## When to wrap up

After the last day in the field. There’s no time limit — you can wrap up the same evening or wait until you’ve confirmed details with your hunters.

## Wrap up

1. Open the trip from your dashboard or trips list.
2. In the action row near the top, tap **Wrap up trip**.
3. The confirmation modal asks **Wrap up this trip?**
4. Tap **Finish trip**.

The trip moves from In progress to Done.

## The hunt log

The Hunt logs page is available at any trip status — before, during, and after wrap-up. Open it from the trip’s action row by tapping **Hunt logs**.

Inside the hunt log:

1. Each hunter on the trip has their own row. Expand a row to fill in their species rows (species, quantity harvested, quantity released, optional notes).
2. Once entries are filled in, scroll to the **Generate filled PDFs** section.
3. Pick the log template you want to fill. The dropdown lists every doc you’ve already mapped at `/app/docs/[id]/mapping`. (See the Hunt logs section of Help once those articles ship.)
4. Tap **Generate Filled Out Log**.

Bite Book builds the PDF using the field mappings you set up. The result lands in the **Logs** list below with these per-row actions:

- **Open** — view the PDF in a new tab.
- **Download** — save it to your device.
- **Sign** — add your signature on top of the existing PDF.
- **Re-generate** — rebuild the PDF if you’ve edited entries since.
- **Delete** — remove this generated copy.

## Hunters and signatures

Hunters cannot sign harvest logs. Only the guide signs the harvest log via the **Sign** action. Hunters can sign **waivers** that you attached to the trip — that’s a separate flow.

## Reopening

A wrapped-up trip is mostly read-only. If you spot a mistake, tap **Reopen trip** in the action row, confirm with **Reopen**, and the trip flips back to In progress so you can edit. Wrap it up again when you’re done.
