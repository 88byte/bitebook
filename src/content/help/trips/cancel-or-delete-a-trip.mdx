Two different actions, two different outcomes. Pick the right one for what you’re trying to do.

## Cancel — for trips that didn’t happen

Cancel a trip when the hunt got called off. Weather, the party backed out, the gate is closed, whatever. The trip stays in your history with a **Canceled** badge so you remember it existed and any waivers or documents stay attached.

To cancel:

1. Open the trip from your dashboard or trips list.
2. In the action row, tap **Cancel trip**.
3. The confirmation modal asks **Cancel this trip?** Tap **Cancel trip** to confirm. (To back out, tap **Keep it**.)

Canceled trips drop out of upcoming/active and don’t generate a state log. The trip and its history stay on file.

## Delete — for trips you should never have created

Delete a trip when you put it in by mistake — wrong state, wrong year, test trip from your first day on Bite Book. Deletion removes the trip and **everything attached to it**.

To delete:

1. Open the trip.
2. In the action row, tap **Delete trip**.
3. The confirmation modal asks you to type the **trip’s name** to confirm. (Case-insensitive — typing matches the literal trip title you set.)
4. Tap **Delete forever**.

Delete removes:

- The trip itself
- Every harvest logged on it
- Every trip-specific link to a hunter (the hunter stays in your network and their licenses and tags stay in their wallet)
- Any generated PDFs of the harvest report
- Any waiver signatures attached to this specific trip

Delete does NOT remove:

- The hunters themselves — they stay in your network and on any other trips they’re part of
- Wallet items they own — their licenses, tags, and permits stay in their account
- Documents in your library that you attached to this trip — the original lives in **Documents**, only the trip-side reference is removed

If you need to delete a trip that you’ve already wrapped up and filed with the state, talk to support first: support@lastbite.pro. We’ll keep a copy of the report on file in case you ever need it.
