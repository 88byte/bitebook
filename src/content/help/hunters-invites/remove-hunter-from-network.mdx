Your hunter network is the list of every hunter you’ve guided or invited. Removing someone from this list is different from removing them from a single trip.

## Remove from a trip vs remove from network

- **Remove from trip** — just takes them off one trip. They stay in your network and you can invite them again. (See: [How to add or remove hunters mid-trip](/app/support/add-remove-hunters-mid-trip).)
- **Remove from network** — takes them off the **Your hunters** list entirely. You won’t see them in the dropdown when inviting hunters to new trips. They keep their own account and any wallet items they own.

## Remove from network

1. Tap **Hunters** in the sidebar.
2. Find the hunter on the **Your hunters** list.
3. On the hunter’s row, tap **Remove from network**.
4. Confirm in the modal.

## What happens

- The hunter disappears from your **Your hunters** list immediately.
- The hunter’s own Bite Book account is **not** deleted. Their license, tags, signed waivers, and trip history all stay in their account.
- Past trips you guided together stay in both accounts. The harvest reports you already filed are unaffected.
- Future trips: the hunter no longer shows up in your invite dropdown. To work with them again, send a fresh email invite from the trip detail page. They’ll be re-added to your network when they accept.

## A hunter who hasn’t accepted yet

**Remove from network** only applies to accepted hunters. If the hunter is still **Pending** (never confirmed their account), use **Cancel invite** on the pending row instead — that stops the invite link from working. The two actions are separate.
