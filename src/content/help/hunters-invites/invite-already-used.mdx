A hunter taps the invite link and sees **This invite link has already been used**. Confusing the first time you see it. Here’s why it happens and what to do.

## The two reasons

**1. Email invites are single-use.** When you send an invite by email, the link is locked to one person. The first hunter to tap it claims it. If you forwarded the same email to two hunters, the second one sees the “already used” message.

**2. The hunter accepted on a different device.** Sometimes a hunter taps the link on their phone, then forgets and taps it again on their laptop. The second tap shows “already used” because they’re already on the trip. They just need to sign in instead.

## How to fix it

### If the wrong person used the link

Send a fresh invite to the right hunter from your **Hunters** page (`/app/hunters`):

1. Open **Hunters** in the sidebar.
2. In the invite form, leave the tab on **Send email**.
3. Type the right hunter’s email.
4. Tap **Send invite**.

Once the right hunter accepts and joins your network, you can add them to the trip via **Manage hunters** on the trip detail.

If the wrong person joined a trip by mistake, open the trip, tap **Manage hunters**, and uncheck their row to remove them from the trip.

### If the hunter is already on the trip

Tell them to sign in at `bitebook.lastbite.pro/login` with the email they originally registered with. The trip is waiting for them on their dashboard. They don’t need a new invite.

### If you want one link that works for multiple people

Use a share link instead of an email invite. Share links are multi-use — anyone with the link can join until you disable it. See: [Email invite vs share link — which to use when](/app/support/email-invite-vs-share-link).
