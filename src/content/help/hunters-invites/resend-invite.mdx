A hunter says they never got your invite. Eight times out of ten it’s in their spam folder. Two times out of ten the email address has a typo. Here’s how to handle both.

## First: have them check spam

Before you resend, ask the hunter to:

1. Open the inbox of the email address you sent the invite to.
2. Check the spam, junk, or promotions folder.
3. Search for **lastbite** — the invite came from `support@lastbite.pro`.

If they find it there, have them tap **Not spam** so future invites land in their inbox.

## Resend the invite

If it’s genuinely missing:

1. Tap the trip.
2. In the **Hunters** section, find the hunter’s row (they’ll show as **Pending**).
3. Tap **Resend invite**.

Bite Book sends a fresh email immediately. To prevent spamming the hunter’s inbox if you tap by mistake, resend is rate-limited to once every 5 minutes per hunter. The button shows a countdown if you’re still in the cooldown window.

## Fix a typo'd email

If you sent the invite to the wrong address:

1. Tap the **Pending** row.
2. Tap **Cancel invite**.
3. Tap **Invite** at the top of the Hunters section.
4. Type the correct email and tap **Send invite**.

The original (wrong-address) link stops working as soon as you cancel.

## Still not landing?

A few rare email providers block transactional mail by default — some corporate Outlook setups and one or two ISPs. If two resends in a row don’t arrive:

1. Switch to a share link instead. Generate one and text it to the hunter directly. (See: [Email invite vs share link](/app/support/email-invite-vs-share-link).)
2. Email us at support@lastbite.pro with the address you tried — we can check our delivery logs.
