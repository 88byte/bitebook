Bite Book gives you two ways to add hunters to a trip. They do the same thing in the end — the hunter lands in their account on the trip detail page. But one is a better fit depending on the situation.

## Email invite

You type a hunter’s email. Bite Book sends them a branded link from your guide account. They tap the link, sign in or sign up, and they’re on the trip.

Use email invites when:

- You have the hunter’s email address
- It’s one or two hunters
- You want a paper trail (the email is timestamped and traceable)

Email invites are **single-use**. Each link works for exactly one hunter and stops working as soon as that hunter accepts it. If you sent the link to the wrong person, see [Why a hunter’s invite says “already used”](/app/support/invite-already-used).

## Share link

You generate one link. You paste it into a text, a group chat, an email of your own, anywhere. Anyone who taps the link and signs in joins the trip.

Use share links when:

- You don’t have everyone’s email yet
- It’s a group of three or more
- You want one link you can paste into a group thread (See: [How to send an invite link to a group of hunters](/app/support/send-invite-link-to-group).)

Share links are **multi-use**. The same link works for as many hunters as need it, until you turn it off. To stop accepting new hunters via the link, tap **Manage hunters** on the trip and tap **Disable share link**.

## Both at the same time

You can use both on the same trip. Send email invites to the two hunters whose addresses you have, and post the share link in the group chat for everyone else. They all end up in the same place.
