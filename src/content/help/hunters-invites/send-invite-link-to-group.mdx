When you’ve got a group of three, four, or more hunters showing up to the same trip, the share link is faster than typing emails one at a time.

## Generate the link

1. Tap the trip.
2. In the **Hunters** section, tap **Invite**.
3. Tap **Share link**.
4. Bite Book generates a link and copies it to your clipboard automatically.

The link looks like `bitebook.lastbite.pro/i/abc123`. Don’t worry about the random characters — that’s the trip-specific token. Each trip gets its own.

## Share it

Paste the link wherever your group is talking:

- A group text
- WhatsApp, Signal, or any other messenger
- An email of your own
- A Facebook group post
- Pinned in your hunting club’s Slack

Each hunter taps the link, signs in or sets a password, and lands on the trip.

## Track who’s joined

Back on the trip detail page, the **Hunters** section updates in real time. Each hunter shows up as soon as they accept. You’ll see:

- **Confirmed** — they’ve fully accepted and their email is verified
- **Pending** — they’ve tapped the link but haven’t finished setting up their account yet

## When you’re done accepting

Once everyone’s joined, kill the link so it can’t be shared further:

1. Tap the trip.
2. Tap **Manage hunters**.
3. Tap **Disable share link**.

Anyone who already accepted stays on the trip. The link itself just stops working for new people.

## A new link for the same trip

If you accidentally posted the link somewhere public and want a clean one, tap **Regenerate link**. The old one stops working immediately and a new token replaces it.
