For groups of hunters showing up to the same trip, share-link mode is faster than typing emails.

## Two kinds of share links

Bite Book has two share-link surfaces:

- **Trip share link** — generated from the trip detail page. The link is tied to that specific trip. Hunters who use it land on the trip immediately. The link expires on the trip’s start date.
- **Network share link** — generated on **Hunters** in your sidebar (`/app/hunters`). The link adds hunters to your network without tying them to a trip. Best when you’re collecting prospective hunters before scheduling anything.

For a known group going on a known trip, use the trip share link.

## Generate a trip share link

1. Open the trip from your dashboard or trips list.
2. Find the **Trip share link** panel and tap **Get trip share link**.
3. Bite Book shows the URL in a read-only field with a **Copy** button next to it.
4. Tap **Copy** to put the URL on your clipboard.

The URL looks like `bitebook.lastbite.pro/accept-invite?token=<long token>`. The token is unique to that trip’s link.

## Share it

Paste the URL anywhere your group is talking — text thread, group chat, email, pinned in a Slack channel. Each hunter taps the link, signs in or sets a password, and lands on the trip.

## Track who joined

The trip’s **Hunters** section updates as people accept. The list reflects the current participants.

## Generating a new link

If you need to rotate the link, tap **Get trip share link** again — it returns the existing active link or generates a new one if none was active. There isn’t a one-tap “disable” or “regenerate” button. The link expires on the trip’s start date regardless.

To stop a link earlier than that, go to your **Hunters** page, find the link row, and tap **Cancel invite**.
